Security & Data Isolation

Last updated: August 17, 2026

LocalShopOps is designed for independently owned Etsy shops. Each seller's authorization and operational data remain separated from other sellers and shops.

OAuth authorization

Etsy access is initiated by the seller and completed directly through Etsy OAuth 2.0. LocalShopOps does not collect or store Etsy passwords. The seller reviews the requested scopes on Etsy before granting access.

Seller and shop boundaries

  • Every Etsy connection is bound to the seller and shop that completed authorization.
  • OAuth tokens, shop data, orders, listings, jobs, exports, permissions, and audit records are shop-scoped.
  • Authorization checks are required before shop data is displayed or an operation is performed.
  • LocalShopOps does not provide cross-seller search, cross-shop listing copying, or multi-shop listing edits.

Minimum access

LocalShopOps requests only the OAuth scopes needed for enabled functions. Listing deletion and buyer email access are not part of current functionality. A change in permissions requires seller re-authorization through Etsy.

Data use restrictions

Etsy data processed by LocalShopOps is used only to provide the service requested by the authorizing seller. It is not:

  • sold or exchanged;
  • shared with another seller;
  • used for third-party advertising;
  • used to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models; or
  • obtained by scraping Etsy webpages.

Operational safeguards

  • Role and shop-based access controls.
  • Explicit user confirmation for Etsy write operations.
  • Audit records for sensitive and external operations.
  • Duplicate-submission controls and validation of write requests.
  • Rate-limit handling and controlled retry behavior.
  • Incident review and notification procedures.

Revocation and deletion

Sellers may revoke Etsy authorization from Etsy's Connected Apps page. They may also contact LocalShopOps to disconnect a shop and request deletion of associated account and operational data. See the disconnect instructions and data deletion process.

Reporting a security concern

Security concerns should be reported to gracecrafted2025@outlook.com or through the Support page.
