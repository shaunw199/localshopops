Security & Data Isolation

Last updated: August 18, 2026

LocalShopOps is designed for independently owned Etsy shops. Each seller's authorization and operational data remain separated from other sellers and shops.

Etsy Open API v3 authorization

Etsy access is initiated by the seller and completed through Etsy Open API v3 using Etsy's OAuth 2.0 Authorization Code flow with PKCE. LocalShopOps does not collect or store Etsy passwords. The seller signs in directly with Etsy and reviews the requested scopes before granting access. Authorization responses are checked against a single-use state value before tokens are accepted.

Seller and shop boundaries

  • Every Etsy connection is bound to the seller and shop that completed authorization.
  • OAuth tokens, shop data, orders, listings, jobs, exports, permissions, and audit records are shop-scoped.
  • Authorization checks are required before shop data is displayed or an operation is performed.
  • LocalShopOps does not provide cross-seller search, cross-shop listing copying, or multi-shop listing edits.

Minimum access

The planned production Commercial Access connection will be limited to transactions_r, listings_r, listings_w, and shops_r for currently planned functions. The listing-deletion scope and access to the buyer_email field are not part of current functionality. Any later increase in access requires a documented product need and seller re-authorization through Etsy.

Data use restrictions

Etsy data processed by LocalShopOps is used only to provide the service requested by the authorizing seller. It is not:

  • sold or exchanged;
  • shared with another seller;
  • used for third-party advertising;
  • used for cross-seller benchmarking, marketplace analytics, or competitive analysis;
  • used to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models; or
  • obtained by scraping Etsy webpages.

Seller-directed supplier exports

Supplier, production, and fulfillment exports require an authorized user action and a seller-selected destination. They may contain only the order or fulfillment fields reasonably necessary for that task. LocalShopOps does not automatically send Etsy member data to suppliers, other sellers, or unrelated third parties.

Commercial data freshness and caching controls

Before broader seller access is opened, LocalShopOps will implement and verify the following controls:

  • Displayed listing content will be refreshed or revalidated within Etsy's six-hour limit.
  • Other displayed Etsy content will be refreshed or revalidated within Etsy's 24-hour limit.
  • Data that cannot be refreshed within the applicable limit will not be presented as current Etsy data.
  • Cached Etsy content will be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the seller-requested service.

Operational safeguards

  • Role and shop-based access controls.
  • Explicit user confirmation for Etsy write operations.
  • Audit records for sensitive and external operations.
  • Duplicate-submission controls and validation of write requests.
  • Rate-limit handling and controlled retry behavior.
  • Incident review and notification procedures.

Commercial launch control

Before broader seller access is opened, LocalShopOps will require an affirmative click-through acceptance of the current Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before an Etsy account can be connected. Acceptance records will include the LocalShopOps account, policy versions, acceptance time, and proportionate security audit information.

Incident notification

If Etsy Member data accessed through the Etsy API is compromised or suspected to be compromised, Grace & Crafted Inc. will notify Etsy at dpo@etsy.com and the affected Etsy seller promptly, and no later than 24 hours after discovery.

Revocation and deletion

Sellers may revoke Etsy authorization from Etsy's Connected Apps page. They may also contact LocalShopOps to disconnect a shop and request deletion of associated account and operational data. See the disconnect instructions and data deletion process.

Reporting a security concern

Security concerns should be reported to gracecrafted2025@outlook.com or through the Support page.
