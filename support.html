Support & Data Requests

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Support is available at gracecrafted2025@outlook.com. Please do not send Etsy passwords, OAuth tokens, buyer addresses, or other unnecessary personal information by email.

Getting help

When contacting support, include your LocalShopOps account identifier, the affected shop name, the approximate time of the issue, and a description of what happened. Remove buyer information and credentials from screenshots.

Etsy reviewer access

Etsy reviewers may request a controlled LocalShopOps demo, test instructions, an interface walkthrough, and technical details about OAuth, data isolation, caching, and write-confirmation controls through the monitored support address. Test credentials and private review instructions are provided directly to authorized reviewers and are not published on this website.

Disconnect LocalShopOps from Etsy

A seller may revoke authorization directly from Etsy:

  1. Sign in to Etsy and open Shop Manager.
  2. Open Apps.
  3. Select the Connected apps tab.
  4. Find LocalShopOps and choose Disconnect.

Revocation prevents future Etsy API access with that authorization. The seller may also contact LocalShopOps support to mark the shop disconnected in LocalShopOps and request deletion of stored OAuth credentials.

Request data deletion

Send a deletion request to gracecrafted2025@outlook.com from the contact address associated with your LocalShopOps account. Include the shop name and state whether you want to delete:

  • the Etsy OAuth connection and tokens;
  • the shop's synchronized operational data;
  • the LocalShopOps seller account; or
  • all of the above.

We may verify the request to protect the seller against unauthorized deletion. After verification, we will delete stored OAuth credentials promptly and delete or de-identify Etsy operational data that is no longer reasonably necessary within 30 days, except limited records that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. We will confirm when the request is complete.

Privacy and security concerns

Mark urgent security reports with “Security” in the subject line. Include only the information necessary to investigate. For privacy questions, access requests, or corrections, use the same monitored support address. If Etsy Member data accessed through the Etsy API is compromised or suspected to be compromised, Grace & Crafted Inc. will notify Etsy and the affected seller promptly, and no later than 24 hours after discovery.
