Seller operations, with each shop in control

Operational tools for independently owned Etsy shops.

LocalShopOps helps Etsy sellers organize authorized order data, review and prepare listings, produce operational reports, and coordinate production and fulfillment—without sharing Etsy passwords.

See how authorization works Review data protections
Availability: LocalShopOps began as a tool for the developer's own shop and is currently used by a limited pilot group of independently owned Etsy sellers. Broader seller availability is pending Etsy Commercial Access approval.

Current purpose

One workspace for seller-authorized operations

Functions are enabled only for the shop and permissions approved by the seller.

OAuth authorization

Each seller connects their own Etsy account

LocalShopOps uses Etsy Open API v3 and Etsy OAuth 2.0. It does not ask sellers to provide Etsy passwords.

1

Seller starts the connection

The seller chooses to connect Etsy from LocalShopOps and is redirected to Etsy's authorization screen.

2

Etsy collects consent

The seller signs in directly with Etsy and reviews the specific permissions requested for enabled features.

3

Access remains shop-scoped

LocalShopOps binds the authorization to that seller and shop. Other sellers cannot access it.

Minimum permissions

Only the access needed for enabled functions

Scopes may vary by feature. A seller must re-authorize if an additional scope is needed.

OAuth scopePurpose
transactions_rRead the authorizing seller's purchase and sales records needed for order operations.
listings_rRead the authorizing seller's private, inactive, or expired listing data.
listings_wCreate or edit seller-authorized listing drafts when that feature is enabled.
shops_rRead necessary information about the Etsy shop connected by the seller.

Not requested for current functions: listing deletion and buyer email access.

Data boundaries

Seller and shop isolation is a core product rule

LocalShopOps is designed so one seller cannot see or operate on another seller's Etsy data.

  • OAuth credentials and connection records are separated by seller and shop.
  • Orders, listings, jobs, permissions, exports, and audit records remain shop-scoped.
  • No cross-seller search, copying, or multi-shop listing modification is provided.
  • Etsy data is not sold, exchanged, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • No Etsy page scraping and no collection of Etsy member passwords.
  • Sellers can revoke Etsy authorization and request deletion of associated LocalShopOps data.
