Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Grace & Crafted Inc. (“LocalShopOps,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) processes information when independently owned Etsy sellers use LocalShopOps.

1. Our role

LocalShopOps acts as a service provider to each Etsy seller that authorizes the application. We process Etsy member and shop data only to provide the services requested by that seller and according to the permissions the seller grants through Etsy OAuth.

2. Information we process

Seller account and support information

Account identifiers, contact details, permission settings, and support communications supplied by the seller.

Etsy authorization information

OAuth access and refresh tokens, granted scopes, Etsy user identifiers, shop identifiers, authorization status, and token lifecycle information. We do not collect Etsy passwords.

Shop and listing information

Shop information, listing identifiers, titles, descriptions, variations, SKUs, images and related listing data needed for seller-authorized listing and operational workflows.

Order, transaction, and fulfillment information

Order identifiers, purchased items, quantities, SKUs, variations, personalization details, order status, and buyer or shipping information only when reasonably necessary to support the seller's order production and fulfillment workflows. LocalShopOps does not request buyer email access for current functions.

Operational and security records

Job status, import and export history, confirmation records, audit events, error reports, and security logs.

3. How we use information

  • connect the seller's authorized Etsy shop;
  • synchronize and display authorized order, sales, shop, and listing data;
  • prepare and execute seller-confirmed listing workflows;
  • create operational, production, supplier, fulfillment, and management reports;
  • maintain seller and shop permissions, data isolation, and auditability;
  • protect LocalShopOps and Etsy users against errors, abuse, and unauthorized access; and
  • provide support and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Seller and shop isolation

LocalShopOps separates OAuth credentials, shop data, orders, listings, permissions, jobs, exports, and audit records by seller and shop. Sellers cannot view, search, copy, or modify another seller's data through LocalShopOps.

5. How we do not use Etsy data

We do not sell or exchange Etsy data. We do not share one seller's data with another seller, use it for third-party advertising, or use it to train AI or machine-learning models. We do not scrape Etsy webpages.

6. Sharing and service providers

We may use infrastructure or technical service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate, secure, or support LocalShopOps. Such providers may process information only for those services and under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

7. Storage, security, and retention

We apply technical and organizational safeguards intended to prevent unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss, or disclosure. We retain Etsy data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain security and audit records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We refresh displayed Etsy information as required and avoid retaining cached Etsy data longer than operationally necessary.

8. Seller choices and controls

Sellers may:

  • decline OAuth authorization;
  • review requested permissions before authorizing LocalShopOps;
  • revoke LocalShopOps from Etsy's Connected Apps page;
  • request access to or correction of their LocalShopOps account information; and
  • request deletion of their account, OAuth credentials, and associated shop data, subject to limited legal or security retention requirements.

Instructions are available on the Data Deletion page.

9. Security incidents

We maintain procedures for investigating suspected unauthorized access or disclosure. When notification is required, we will notify affected sellers, Etsy, and relevant authorities within the applicable time requirements.

10. International processing

Depending on how a seller accesses LocalShopOps and where supporting infrastructure is located, information may be processed in a country different from the seller's country. We apply safeguards required by applicable law.

11. Policy updates

We may update this policy when LocalShopOps features, legal obligations, or data practices change. Material changes will be communicated through the application, website, or seller contact information as appropriate.
